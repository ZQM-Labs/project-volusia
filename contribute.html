Contribution Types

  • tool — Software, scripts, data fetchers
  • data_source — New public data sources
  • analysis — Research or data analysis
  • community — Local knowledge and observations
  • direct — Strategic feedback and mission input

Review Process

  • All submissions are reviewed by the Project Volusia Governance Board within 5 business days.
  • You will receive an update at your provided email (if any).
  • Approved contributions are integrated into the codebase or knowledge base.
  • You can check status anytime using your submission ID.

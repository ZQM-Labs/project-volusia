REPORT TEMPLATES — PROJECT VOLUSIA
====================================
Version: 1.0 | Date: 2026-09-03
Owner: Project Volusia Report Lead
Classification: Internal — Public-Facing Standard

---

1. PURPOSE
==========

This document defines the templates for all Project Volusia public-facing
reports. Every report follows one of these templates to ensure consistency,
accessibility, and standards compliance.

---

2. REPORT TYPES
=================

2.1 QUARTERLY ECONOMIC BRIEFING
---------------------------------
  AUDIENCE: Business owners, industry movers, government
  FREQUENCY: Quarterly (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec)
  LENGTH: 8-12 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. Executive Summary (1 page)
    2. Key Indicators Dashboard (1 page, visual)
    3. Employment & Labor Market (2 pages)
    4. Industry Performance (2 pages)
    5. Real Estate & Construction (1-2 pages)
    6. Tourism Economy (1-2 pages)
    7. Outlook & Risks (1 page)
    8. Data Sources & Methodology (1 page, appendix)

2.2 MONTHLY TOURISM UPDATE
----------------------------
  AUDIENCE: Tourism industry, CVB, hospitality operators
  FREQUENCY: Monthly
  LENGTH: 2-4 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. Headline Indicators (visitor volume, occupancy, bed tax)
    2. Trend vs. Prior Year
    3. Events Calendar Impact
    4. Emerging Issues (social media signals, weather, etc.)

3. ANNUAL STATE OF THE COUNTY
-------------------------------
  AUDIENCE: All stakeholders, public
  FREQUENCY: Annual (January)
  LENGTH: 20-30 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. Letter from Project Leadership (1 page)
    2. Demographic Profile (3 pages)
    3. Economic Profile (4 pages)
    4. Tourism Profile (3 pages)
    5. Education & Workforce (2 pages)
    6. Health & Well-Being (2 pages)
    7. Infrastructure & Environment (2 pages)
    8. Equity Analysis (2 pages)
    9. Strategic Outlook (2 pages)
    10. Data Appendix (remaining pages)

4. BUSINESS OWNER TOOLKIT
---------------------------
  AUDIENCE: Small and mid-market business owners
  FREQUENCY: Quarterly (aligned with economic briefing)
  LENGTH: 4-6 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. Market Snapshot (local demand, competition)
    2. Pricing & Wage Benchmarks
    3. Regulatory Updates (permitting, licensing)
    4. Resources & Programs (grants, training)

5. TOURIST EXPERIENCE REPORT
------------------------------
  AUDIENCE: Tourists, CVB, hospitality industry
  FREQUENCY: Semi-annual (pre-season and post-season)
  LENGTH: 6-10 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. What to Expect (weather, events, conditions)
    2. Where to Go (attractions, beaches, nature)
    3. Honest Reviews (aggregated sentiment)
    4. Practical Info (parking, transit, safety)

6. RESIDENT WELL-BEING REPORT
-------------------------------
  AUDIENCE: Residents, community organizations, government
  FREQUENCY: Annual
  LENGTH: 10-15 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. Cost of Living & Affordability
    2. Employment & Wages
    3. Education & Schools
    4. Health & Healthcare Access
    5. Public Safety
    6. Environment & Climate
    7. Civic Engagement

7. INDUSTRY MOVER INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
--------------------------------------
  AUDIENCE: Investors, developers, economic development
  FREQUENCY: Quarterly
  LENGTH: 4-6 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. Investment Climate (capital flow, permitting velocity)
    2. Industry Mix & Trends
    3. Workforce Availability
    4. Infrastructure Capacity
    5. Competitive Positioning

8. SPECIAL REPORTS / EMERGENCY BRIEFS
----------------------------------------
  AUDIENCE: All stakeholders
  FREQUENCY: As needed (hurricane, economic shock, major event)
  LENGTH: 1-4 pages
  SECTIONS:
    1. What Happened
    2. Current Conditions (with uncertainty labels)
    3. Impact Assessment (preliminary)
    4. What We're Watching
    5. Where to Get Help

---

3. VISUAL STANDARDS
=====================

  - Color palette: Wong color-blind accessible palette
  - Font: System fonts (Arial, Helvetica, or similar)
  - Chart Y-axis: Starts at 0 (unless clearly labeled otherwise)
  - Maps: EPSG:4269 or EPSG:2236, with source citation
  - All charts include: title, source, vintage, axis labels
  - All maps include: legend, scale bar, north arrow, source citation

---

4. READING LEVEL
==================

Public-facing summaries: 8th-grade reading level (Flesch-Kincaid).
Technical appendices are exempt.

---

5. APPROVAL WORKFLOW
======================

  1. Analyst drafts report using template
  2. Report Lead reviews for standards compliance
  3. Data Lead verifies data accuracy
  4. Methodologist verifies methodology
  5. Report Lead signs off
  6. Executive Sponsor approves public release (Tier 4)
